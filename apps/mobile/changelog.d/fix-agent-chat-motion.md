### Fixed

- Android chats with ColorOS motion on now glide to a new reply line, a new row and a changed activity row after you send a message. Before, the list jumped to the end in one frame each time the reply grew.
- When a reply finishes in an Android chat with ColorOS motion on, the message actions now show in the same frame that the activity row goes. Before, the chat moved up and then down again 260 ms later.
- The quote of a sent reply now rises with its bubble in Android chats with ColorOS motion on. Before, the quote showed at once and the bubble rose under it.

### Changed

- In Android chats with ColorOS motion on, the message actions and the turn steps row fade in when a reply finishes, a failed message shows its error with a short fade, and the "Waiting" line of a held message fades out.
