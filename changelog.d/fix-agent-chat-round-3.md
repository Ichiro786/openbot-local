### Changed

- The OpenBot Local engine no longer sends your reaction to an agent's reply as a new message. The reaction shows only on the message that you reacted to, and the chat gets no emoji message from you and no emoji answer from the agent. Before, engine 0.74.0 sent the emoji as your reply to that message, so one reaction added two rows to the chat. A reaction no longer lets the agent continue in place of a typed answer. Reactions that you added before this change stay in the chat history.
